# Topical Authority — aquarium.kaca.co.id

## Role and boundary

`aquarium.kaca.co.id` is a specialist Indonesian knowledge and commercial-support property for planning, specifying, installing, operating, and maintaining glass aquariums for freshwater and marine use. It should help homeowners, aquascapers, facility teams, and buyers make evidence-based decisions without replacing structural design, glazing expertise, electrical work, water-quality testing, or aquatic-animal care. Existing design, diagnostic, contact, and custom-order routes retain their current intent. Glass type and thickness, water pressure and structural load, seams and adhesives, support/base capacity, filtration and water-system claims, installation safety, maintenance, standards, warranty, prices, and livestock suitability require project-specific calculations or tests, current primary evidence, and competent professional review before publication.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links upward to its AKU topic hub. AKU-01 routes readers through glass/structure/joints/support (AKU-02–AKU-06), installation (AKU-07), life-support systems (AKU-08–AKU-11), display/environment (AKU-12/AKU-13), and lifecycle/procurement evidence (AKU-14–AKU-16). Maintenance pages link to the relevant existing symptom route when a specific condition appears, then back to prevention and repair criteria. Commercial calls to action point to `/desain/kaca-custom/` or `/kontak-kami/`; no knowledge page duplicates a location template or promises livestock suitability from tank volume alone.
